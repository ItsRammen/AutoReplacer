# Upstream

This folder is an unmodified import of **Open Headunit**:

- Repository: https://github.com/andreknieriem/open-headunit
- Commit: `ae74788d8312dd7a735775ddc21370f1741a7af1` (2026-11-03)
- Licence: GNU Affero General Public License v3 (`LICENSE`), with Michael A. Reid's original headunit notice (`COPYRIGHT_MICHAEL_REID_GPLv3AFFERO.txt`).

AutoReplacer builds its Auto+ Show (Garmin USB Link) head unit on this code. Changes made here are AutoReplacer's own commits on top of this import, so they can later be replayed onto a GitHub fork of Open Headunit, for example with `git format-patch --relative=openheadunit <import-commit>..`.

Any distributed build derived from this folder must be released under the AGPL v3 with its complete corresponding source.

## AutoReplacer changes on top of the import

The import commit is unmodified upstream. Later commits change (so they can be replayed onto a fork):

- `app/build.gradle.kts`: `applicationIdSuffix = ".autoreplacer"` on the `github` flavour.
- `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`: the `NAVIGATION_UPDATE` custom permission name now starts with `${applicationId}` (a fixed name made Android refuse to install next to the original app, INSTALL_FAILED_DUPLICATE_PERMISSION); removed the root, Bluetooth scan/advertise and external-storage permissions; added `autoshow.AutoShowLauncherActivity` (USB accessory attach) and `autoshow.AutoShowService`.
- `app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml`: "Self Mode" labels are now "Auto+ Show mode" and the app is named AutoReplacer (English only; translations are untouched).
- `utils/Settings.kt`: an unset connection choice defaults to Self (Auto+ Show) mode only.
- New package `app/src/main/java/com/andrerinas/openheadunit/autoshow/` and `res/xml/autoshow_accessory_filter.xml`.
- `autoshow-tests/`: plain-Java checks for the copied Garmin link classes (`run.sh`).
- `main/OnboardingActivity.kt` and `main/SettingsFragment.kt`: one-line hooks into `autoshow/AutoShowUi.kt`, which hides the connection-type, video-test, display, DPI, appearance, sound, automation, car-launcher, location and vehicle wizard steps, the Wireless / Automation / Input / Navigation / Theming / More features settings sections, a few individual display and USB settings, and the "change connection types" note. Set `AutoShowUi.ENABLED = false` to restore the original screens. Welcome text strings are reworded.
- `utils/AppPermissions.kt`: the "Nearby devices" row asks only for `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` (scan/advertise are no longer in the manifest, so requiring them left the row permanently ungranted), and the overlay, write-settings and storage rows are hidden.
- `autoshow/AutoShowReadiness.kt`: plain-words setup checks (Android Auto installed, Developer options, USB debugging, notifications) shown in the wizard's final summary; `AutoShowService` also puts a USB-debugging reminder in its notification.
- `autoshow/CarBluetooth.kt`, `BluetoothPlan.java`, `Wire.carBluetoothAddress`: reads the car's Bluetooth address from VIDEO_READY (bytes 44-60), starts pairing if the phone is not bonded to it, and reports whether the car has connected media/call audio. Public APIs only; logs show just the last two address bytes.
- Branding: `main/AboutFragment.kt` (AutoReplacer header, repository link and a "Built on Open Headunit / Headunit by Michael A. Reid" credit above upstream's changelog; the AGPL text stays), English strings for the name, About, copyright line, notification and disclaimer, `main/RenameNotice.kt` and `main/Aa174Notice.kt` (upstream notices suppressed), and a new adaptive launcher icon (`res/drawable/ic_autoreplacer_foreground.xml`, `res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher*.xml`, `res/values/autoreplacer_colors.xml`). Strings of hidden features and non-English translations still say Open Headunit or Self Mode.
- `autoshow/AutoShowUi.kt` also hides, in sections that stay: auto-optimize, video codec/narrow-band/software decoder/low-latency, all media-sink audio tuning, the performance overlay, fault-injection debug tools, and upstream's update check and report-a-problem entries.
- Car-session guards: `decoder/video/VideoDecoder.kt` gets `surfaceLock` (while a car session runs, `setSurface` ignores every Surface except the car encoder's); `aap/AapProjectionActivity.kt` finishes immediately and `aap/AapService.kt` does not launch it while `AutoShowService.carSessionActive`. Without these, opening the app on the phone raised Open Headunit's projection screen, took the decoder away from the car encoder and left the car blank.
- `AutoShowService`: `AUTOSHOW_STATS` lines (every 5 s for a minute, then every 30 s) and a "Save log" notification action that writes this app's log to Downloads.
- `utils/HeadUnitScreenConfig.kt`: `carCanvas` override (landscape car size). While set, Android Auto is told to draw for the car's screen instead of the phone's; without it a portrait phone produced a 720x1280 portrait canvas with margins.
- `main/MainActivity.kt`: does not raise the projection screen while a car session is active (together with the guard in `AapProjectionActivity` this was an open/close loop of thousands of launches per second).
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt`: starts `CommManager.startReading()` once the handshake completes. Open Headunit normally waits for its projection screen to do this; with no screen, Android Auto waited forever for a service-discovery answer and sent no video. Also nudges video focus if no picture has arrived 6 s after the transport starts.
- `autoshow/AutoShowState.kt`, `AutoShowStatusCard.kt`, `AutoShowLogSaver.kt`; `main/HomeFragment.kt`: the home screen's main button shows the car session (waiting / connecting / Android Auto starting / showing on the car / disconnected, plus touch and Bluetooth lines) instead of offering to start a connection. USB, Wi-Fi and Auto+ Show-mode tiles are hidden; Reconnect appears when the cable is in and no session runs.
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt`: a replug while the previous session is still closing reuses the running service instead of being ignored (`stopSelf(startId)`, `resetForNewSession`).
- `autoshow/CarSplash.java`, `AutoShowService.kt`: a waiting picture (spinner, app name, current step, seconds) is sent to the car from a second encoder until the first Android Auto picture leaves the main encoder. `AUTOSHOW_STATS` now also reports `aaPictureFrames` and `splash` because `carFrames` counts the waiting picture too.
- `autoshow/AutoShowServerProbe.kt`, `AutoShowStatusCard.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`: warns (home card, notification, car waiting picture) when nothing listens on Android Auto's head unit server port 127.0.0.1:5277, and retries the Android Auto connection every 6 s (up to 100 times) instead of giving up after one failed handshake. `CarSplash` wraps long lines.
- Splash (`activity_main.xml` overlay, `splash_background.xml`, `splash_background`/`splash_text_revived` colours): AutoReplacer icon and name on the icon's dark background instead of the Open Headunit logo. The server warning on the home card is amber with its own title ("Start Android Auto's server first").
- Launcher icon and splash mark (`ic_autoreplacer_foreground.xml`): a steering wheel with a phone at the hub, replacing the screen-with-play-arrow that looked like a video app.
- Notification: the foreground service title is the app name (was a hardcoded "Open Headunit"), its channel is "AutoReplacer service", the connected text reads "Android Auto is connected", and the default vehicle display name sent to Android Auto is "AutoReplacer".
- `AutoShowStatusCard`: the home card shows only status; Reconnect is the one button and appears only when usable; Save log is in a ⋮ menu in the card corner; favorite apps, widgets row, page dots and the apps button are hidden.
- `AutoShowService`: `AUTOSHOW_TOUCH_RESUMED` (first touch after 8 s of silence), `PHONE_STATE` lines for screen off/on/unlock, and `touchLines` / `touchesDropped` / `lastTouch` in `AUTOSHOW_STATS`, to find why car touch stops with the phone locked or another app open.
- `aap/AapMessageHandlerType.kt`: in car mode logs `AA_MSG channel type size` for every inbound message except video and audio data (first 800 per connection), to see what Android Auto does after a tap on its bottom bar.
- `autoshow/TouchArea.java`, `AutoShowTouchArea.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`, `AutoShowStatusCard.kt`: the car's touch coordinates can run past the picture size it declares (y up to ~795 for 1280x720). The touch extent is learned from the largest coordinate seen (rounded up to a common panel size, kept between sessions), or chosen from the ⋮ menu (Auto / picture height / 800), and car touches are scaled into the Android Auto picture instead of clamped. Logged as `AUTOSHOW_TOUCH_AREA`.
- `autoshow/AutoShowNotification.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`, `aap/AapService.kt`: one notification (id 1, the default channel) for the whole app. The car session and Android Auto's own service both post it while a car session runs, showing the step and the touch and Bluetooth lines, with Save log; tapping it opens the app. The separate "Auto+ Show" channel is gone.
- `connection/self/launchers/SelfLauncherV17_4.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`, `AutoShowStatusCard.kt`: in car mode a failed head unit server connection no longer toasts and opens Android Auto settings on every retry. Android Auto settings are opened once per session (best effort) and from an "Open Android Auto" button on the warning card.
- `autoshow/AutoShowTouchHub.kt`, `CarTouchServer.java`, `App.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`: the port-8051 server now starts with the app process and lives as long as it, so the car's own touch helper cannot take the port after a session ends or on a cold start. If the port is already held, the server sends it one line it cannot parse (`a=ts x=exit y=exit b=exit`): the helper's server loop ends on that exception and the helper exits (read when the car's connection closes at the latest). Tested against a copy of the helper loop (`CarTouchServerTest`).
- `autoshow/CarBluetooth.kt`: the Bluetooth line follows the car live (A2DP / headset connection-state, ACL and bond broadcasts for the car's address, plus a 15 s / 60 s re-check) instead of checking for 20 s after plug-in and stopping. When the car is paired but an audio profile is not connected, after 6 s it asks Android to connect it by reflection (up to 3 tries; the call needs BLUETOOTH_PRIVILEGED on most phones, so the result is logged as `CAR_BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`).
- `autoshow/CarCalibrationScreen.java`, `TouchArea.fit/unmap`, `AutoShowTouchArea.kt`, `AutoShowService.kt`, `CarEncoder.java`: a guided touch calibration (⋮ menu → Calibrate touch on car). Nine crosses are drawn on the car by a separate encoder while Android Auto's picture waits; the taps are fitted per axis (measured = a * picture + b) and the mapping is saved and used instead of the learned extent. Android Auto's picture resumes with its stored SPS/PPS and a requested key frame.
- `autoshow/AutoShowEventLog.kt`, `AutoShowLogSaver.kt`: every AutoShow line is also kept in an app-owned file (1.5 MB cap) and appended to the saved log, because the system log only holds the last few minutes of a long session.
- Touch mapping: the user-facing Auto / 720 / 800 choice is gone. Until calibrated the app uses a silent estimate (largest coordinate seen); after Calibrate touch on car the measured mapping is used. Settings → Car touch shows the state ("Calibrated (width …, height …)" or "Not calibrated") with Touch calibration and Reset touch calibration.
- `autoshow/PortListening.java`, `AutoShowServerProbe.kt`: the head unit server check no longer connects to 127.0.0.1:5277 (Android Auto's server takes one connection per start, and a connect-and-close probe used it up: "the peer accepted the connection and then sent nothing" on the real session). It tries to bind the port instead, which fails exactly when a listener holds it.
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt` (`enterStandby`, `resumeFromStandby`): when the car link drops while Android Auto's picture is flowing, Android Auto, the decoder and the encoder are kept for 90 s (new `Phase.STANDBY`). A returning cable rebuilds only the car link: the stored SPS/PPS and a key frame go out, no Android Auto restart, no head unit server restart. A different car picture size or the timeout ends the session.
- `autoshow/DisconnectReason.java`: plain-language text for the car link's failure reasons (the technical reason is logged as `AUTOSHOW_LINK_ENDED`). The card, notification and standby text use it.
- Touch calibration protection: `AutoShowTouchArea` keeps the previous calibration (`rememberPrevious`, `restorePrevious`); Settings → Car touch asks before resetting or recalibrating and offers "Restore previous calibration".
- `CHANGELOG-AUTOREPLACER.md` (new, bundled as an asset), `AboutFragment.kt`, `app/build.gradle.kts`: About shows AutoReplacer's own user-facing changelog and links to Open Headunit's upstream one instead of embedding it; versionName is now AutoReplacer's (0.7.6, versionCode unchanged). Keep the new file up to date with each release.
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt` (`rebuildEncoder`, `mainSink`): when Android Auto's session ends mid-car-session the car encoder is replaced and the decoder re-attached to the new input surface. Found in the field: after Android Auto reconnected, `GraphicBufferSource: Dropping frame that's going backward in time` discarded every frame, because the new decoder's timestamps restart from zero. On resume the car gets the waiting picture until Android Auto's first new frame; Android Auto retries after 1.5 s (not 6 s) when it had been running; a car link that is not video-ready is replaced by a new accessory attach instead of the attach being ignored (`GarminLink.isVideoReady`); a link's late end report is ignored unless it is the current link.
- `autoshow/GarminLink.java`, `AutoShowService.kt`, `AutoShowNotification.kt`: the car's driving-restriction flag is shown. A session that starts with `restricted=1` never gets the car's touch (the car withholds it for apps it has not allowed), so the Touch line, card and notification say "Locked by the car" and clear when the car reports no restriction. The app still answers "allowed" only for an unrestricted, stationary car, and still ends the session when the car reports motion; it does not override the restriction.
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt`, `AutoShowState.kt`: the Touch line says "Waiting for the car" / "Connected; waiting for the first touch" (on `TS8051_CONNECTED`) / "Working (car screen WxH)" (first touch), and the notification re-posts on every `AutoShowState` change. It used to stay at "Not connected yet" until the first touch, because only `setStatus` refreshed the notification.
- `autoshow/GarminLink.java`, `AutoShowService.kt`: `sendVideo` drops buffers until the car's VIDEO_READY (in standby the encoder kept feeding a new link before the car was ready, and the car reset its USB: `GARMIN_FIRST_VIDEO_BUFFER_SENT` before `GARMIN_VIDEO_READY`, then `write failed: EIO` within ~5 s); `abort()` closes a replaced link at once (its normal stop waits a second, so the next link got `IllegalStateException: Input stream already open for this accessory`); opening the accessory retries up to six times on that error (`GARMIN_USB_BUSY`).
- Correction to the 0.7.8/0.7.9 entries above: the "Locked by the car" Touch status is removed (0.7.12). Logs show touch working with `restricted=1` and no reply (15:22:18 flag set, `a=init` at 15:22:27, touches to 15:23:48), and `speed` is 0 in every state including while driving, so the flag does not gate touch reliably. It is only logged (`AUTOSHOW_CAR_RESTRICTION_FLAG`). The reply policy (`DiagnosticPolicy`) is unchanged.
- `autoshow/AutoShowService.kt`: the event log also records `POWER_CONNECTED` / `POWER_DISCONNECTED`, `USB_STATE` (with its extras) and `USB_ACCESSORY_DETACHED` as `PHONE_STATE` lines, to tell a pulled cable from a lost data link.
- `autoshow/GarminLink.java`: a ring of the last 48 messages (both directions; video folded to one line per second) is written to the event log when the link fails: `GARMIN_DROP_CONTEXT` (time open, time since last receive/send, write in progress, buffers sent) and `GARMIN_LAST_TRAFFIC` lines.
